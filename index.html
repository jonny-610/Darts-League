Privacy & security

This site is in beta and run by volunteers for the league. Here's what we keep, who can see it and how it's protected.

What we store

  • Your name as you entered it, your division and starting 3-dart average.
  • Match results and the match stats you or your opponent enter, plus any screenshots uploaded as proof.
  • Your player code, kept in a private part of the database that only admins can read.
  • Reports you send with "Report a problem", and technical error messages from the page.
  • We don't store email addresses, phone numbers, locations or passwords for players, and there is no tracking or advertising.

Who can see it

  • Names, tables, results, stats and tournament draws are public to anyone with the link.
  • Screenshots can be opened by anyone who has their link. Don't upload anything showing faces, addresses or anything else private.
  • Player codes, admin passwords, the admin log and problem reports are only visible to the league admins.

How it's protected

  • The site is served over HTTPS (encrypted) and only runs its own code: no third-party scripts, trackers or adverts.
  • Every change is checked on the server: the league code, your player code or an admin password is required, and you can only submit results for your own matches.
  • 10 wrong codes in an hour locks that connection out for an hour.
  • Admin passwords are stored scrambled (hashed). Every admin action is logged, and undoing one needs a second admin to agree.
  • Results are never wiped: removed results are hidden and can be restored. Admins download backups at the end of each season.
  • Your opponent confirms each result, and disputed results don't count until an admin checks the proof.

Your choices

  • You can ask an admin to correct your name or details, withdraw you, or remove your screenshots at any time.
  • To have your data deleted, ask an admin. Results may be kept with your name removed so other players' tables stay correct.
  • Spotted a security problem? Use "Report a problem" or tell an admin directly. Please don't test it on the live site.

Match format

  • 501, straight in, double out. Best of 8 legs: first to 5 wins; 4-4 is a draw.
  • Player A throws first in leg 1 (or throw for bull). The throw alternates every leg.
  • Everyone in a division plays everyone else twice. Win 3 pts, draw 1, loss 0.
  • League dates are set by the admin each season.

Submitting

  • The winner submits, one row per match. On a draw Player A submits.
  • Played on our Scorer? Submit from the Scorer and the stats are saved for you; no screenshot needed.
  • Played in DartCounter or another app? Use Result entry: date, names, legs, every match stat for both players, and screenshots of the app's Statistics and Match details screens. See the example below.
  • Player code: every player has a 4-character code. New players get theirs when they join; existing players get theirs from an admin. Save it once at the top of the page and it's remembered on that phone. You can only submit results for matches you played in.
  • Screenshot rule: if you played in DartCounter or any other app, upload screenshots of the Statistics and Match details screens (like the example below) showing both players' names and the score. They're required for app results. Results without proof can be removed by the admin. Matches scored on our Scorer don't need one.
  • Confirming: after you submit, your opponent confirms the result on their My matches page. If it's wrong they can dispute it within 48 hours, and it won't count until an admin checks the screenshots and decides. No response in 48 hours = confirmed.
  • The Status column must say OK, otherwise the row is ignored.

If matches aren't played

  • Unplayed matches are void: no points to either player, no penalty.
  • Tables are ranked by points per game, so players who play fewer games aren't punished.
  • Play-by dates: My matches suggests a "play by" date for each opponent so games are spread across the season. It's a guide, not a deadline: late matches still count, and nobody loses points for them.
  • Tap any player's name to see their profile: results, averages, head-to-heads and season history.
  • To take a playoff place you must play at least 50% of your fixtures. Top 2 qualifiers per division go through.
  • A withdrawn player's matches are all voided and they leave the table.

Tournaments

  • 8 to 32 players. Anyone with the league code can put their name down on the Tournaments page until the admin closes entries.
  • Random draw into groups of 3 or 4. Everyone in a group plays each other once. Ranked by wins, then leg difference, then legs won.
  • Top 2 from each group go into a random knockout draw. Byes go through automatically when numbers don't fill the bracket.
  • The admin sets how many legs to win in the groups, the knockout and the final.
  • Play each match on the live scorer (one phone or online across two) or in any darts app, then tap Play / enter result. Either player can submit; a match can only be submitted once.
